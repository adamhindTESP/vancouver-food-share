BC safety guidance, checked: https://www.bccdc.ca/health-professionals/professional-resources/food-donation-guidelines
Its refrigerated-perishable-food decision chart was downloaded and visually reviewed:
https://www.bccdc.ca/resource-gallery/Documents/Educational%20Materials/EH/FPS/Food/refrigerated_perishable_food_decision_tree.pdf
The chart requires temperature assessment, refrigeration, and appropriate disposition when temperature history is unsafe/unknown. Candidate guidance is deliberately conservative: maintain cold storage, do not leave perishables in an unrefrigerated box, and do not donate food of uncertain safety. This is not a food-safety certification.
