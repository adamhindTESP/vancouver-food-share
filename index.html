Donation source pages checked 4 November 2026. Get permission and confirm suitability before bringing food.

1

Check the location’s rules

Read the linked organizer guidance and posted signs. If accepted items or access are unclear, confirm with the organizer before bringing food.

2

Keep food safe

Follow safe handling and storage guidance. Keep chilled food cold during transport; never leave perishables in an unrefrigerated donation box.

3

Leave it in the right place

Use the designated storage only. If it is full, closed or unsuitable for your food, take the food with you. Respect the space and neighbours.

Unsure about food safety? Don’t donate food whose storage history or safety is uncertain. Follow the location’s rules alongside BC Centre for Disease Control food donation guidance.

Guidance source checked 16 October 2026. Host acceptance does not by itself establish that an item is safe.

What to check before sharing your version
  • Start with existing directories and verify local locations, entrances, hours, food rules and registration requirements. Record what you checked and when.
  • Use your own contact information and check local food-safety, legal and hosting requirements. Get consent before listing a new host or private address.
  • Confirm your own public URL before making your own QR code. Keep the MIT license and copyright notice.

Copying the website does not create a food program, supply boxes or establish a partnership.

Thinking about hosting?

Interested in hosting a food-sharing box?

We’re exploring small food-sharing boxes with local businesses and community spaces. Contact Vancouver Food Share to express interest. This is an early conversation, not a commitment: no box, launch date or placement is promised.

Practical arrangements and responsibilities would need to be agreed before anything goes ahead. Prospective hosts and their addresses are not listed here.

Ask about hosting →

This opens your email app. Nothing is submitted until you send the email. You can also email Vancouverfoodshare.contact@gmail.com. Please don’t include personal health, income or identity documents.

A host needs to agree to access, accepted food, cleaning, checks, storage and removal of unsuitable items. An unrefrigerated box is not suitable for perishable food.

Questions to settle before listing a host
  • Has the host agreed to a public listing and address?
  • Who checks, cleans and maintains the space?
  • What food is accepted, and how must it be labelled?
  • What are the public access hours and entrance?
  • Who can confirm changes or temporary closure?
